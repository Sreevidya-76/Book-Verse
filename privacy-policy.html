Privacy Policy

How we handle information for this reader-focused project.

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Information we collect

Book Verse may collect account details, saved books, order information, and messages you choose to submit. We only use this information to provide and improve the experience.

How we use information

We use information to manage your library, respond to support requests, personalize discovery, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You may review or update your account details, request help with your information, or contact us at hello@bookverse.example.

Data security

We use reasonable safeguards for the information handled by this project. No online service can promise absolute security.
